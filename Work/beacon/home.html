Case Study

Beacon

Role

Product Designer

Timeline

16 weeks

Team

UofT & Vector Institute

Manual contact tracing is too slow to keep up with rapidly spreading outbreaks. Beacon bridges the gap between technology and public health by automating tracing with privacy-first tools that help protect communities while preserving civil liberties.

Problem

Understanding
our Goal

Contact tracing helps identify and notify people exposed to infectious diseases, but manual methods are slow and depend on memory. During COVID-19, these limitations became clear.

Our goal was to design Beacon, a digital contact tracing app that uses smartphone proximity tracking to make tracing faster, more scalable, and easier for both the public and health officials.

Secondary Market Research

Data-Driven Strategy

We researched global contact tracing apps and Canadian government studies to understand adoption and user needs.Our research found that privacy concerns were the biggest barrier, while older people were surprisingly more willing to adopt tracing apps. We also identified poor healthcare integration and unclear guidance as major weaknesses.

Technology Feasibility

Privacy-First Proximity Tracking

We evaluated GPS and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for contact tracing. While GPS provides location context, it lacks accuracy in many real-world situations and raises privacy concerns. We chose BLE because it accurately detects nearby devices without collecting location data, making it both more reliable and more privacy-friendly.

The Apple/Google API

Overcoming OS Limitations

Early BLE implementations were limited because iOS required apps to remain active, and Android/iOS communication was inconsistent. Apple and Google solved this with a shared Exposure Notification API that enabled reliable background tracing while maintaining strong privacy protections.

Competitive Analysis

Identifying Market Gaps

We analyzed existing contact tracing apps to understand why adoption remained low. Most competitors successfully used BLE and the Apple/Google API but stopped at exposure notifications, offering little guidance or healthcare support afterward.

Insights

User Personas

Using research and storyboarding, we created personas that reflected a wide range of users, with a particular focus on older adults. This reinforced the need for a simple, accessible experience that worked for everyone.

Storyboarding

Designing for the Vulnerable

We mapped user journeys to understand how people would interact with Beacon during stressful situations. Rather than simply notifying users of exposure, we designed an experience that immediately connects them with guidance and healthcare resources.

Problem Definition

Bridging the Gap

Our research highlighted three core problems: privacy concerns, technical reliability, and limited healthcare integration. Existing apps stopped at notifying users. Beacon extends the journey by helping users navigate the next steps after exposure.

Problem Definition

Our Solution

Beacon builds on the Apple/Google framework by integrating directly with public health services. Instead of only notifying users of exposure, it connects them with guidance while protecting privacy and personal data.

Iterations & Testing

From Wireframes to High-Fidelity

We started with low-fidelity wireframes before refining the experience through three rounds of user testing and iteration. Testing with 12 participants resulted in a 100% task completion rate and a 91.7% error-free rate.

Design System

Visual Foundation

Building Trust Immediately

The onboarding flow clearly explains privacy and value from the start. Users only provide their city, allowing Beacon to connect them with their local Public Health Unit without collecting personally identifiable information.

The Core Experience

Beacon centers around three core screens: a dashboard for exposure updates, local health statistics, and an account page where users manage their data and settings.

Intelligent Risk Assessment

A built-in self-assessment evaluates a user's level of exposure and provides personalized guidance. This helps users make informed decisions while reducing unnecessary strain on healthcare services.

Stress-Free Reporting

Users who test positive can securely report their diagnosis using a verification code from their Public Health Unit. Beacon then shifts from a tracking tool into a recovery resource, providing personalized guidance and next steps.

Learnings & Outcome

A Reflection on the Experience

Designing for millions of Canadians reinforced the importance of trust, accessibility, and privacy. By simplifying complex technology, we created an experience that felt secure and approachable.

Although Canada's official COVID Alert app followed a different path, seeing several of our ideas reflected in the final product validated our approach. The project also taught me the importance of moving quickly and adapting in fast-changing environments.
