About Me

I’m currently leading design at Wondera, building the music platform for the AI age. Before that, I co-founded Beavr Design, where I helped startups grow and achieve design excellence. Earlier in my career, I collaborated with ML researchers from Stanford and Harvard to transform the IP industry, and held roles at Twitch, Robinhood, Shopify, Instagram, and the Schulich School of Medicine. During the pandemic, I worked with doctors and professors to create a contact-tracing app, which we presented to the provincial and federal governments of Canada.

When it comes to my work, I strive to create solutions that address real-world problems and leave a lasting impact on society. Design and code have been staples of my life since childhood. It all started with me trying to develop my own Pokèmon game and designing the interior of cars. Little did I know that passion would carry through to where I am today.

I love spending time with family and friends, and I deeply value positivity and gratefulness as guiding principles in life. I’m always excited to connect with new people, so feel free to reach out to me on LinkedIn or via email—I’d love to hear from you!
