The Options Chain

The Options Chain is a listing of all available options contracts for a given security. It shows all listed calls, puts, their expiration, strike prices, and volume and pricing information for a single underlying asset within a given maturity period. Robinhood's option chain page had not evolved in a long time. User needs shifted and there were improvements that could be made to the data displayed for each contract. I redesigned the mobile chain and bottom sheet with multiple data models, testing how different information structures improved usability and decision-making.
