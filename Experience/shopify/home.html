Plugin’ Polaris into Figma

The Polaris team supports designers and developers across multiple tools, but each experience felt disconnected. Our goal was to create a unified ecosystem where crafters, whether using the API, website, VS Code, or Figma interacted with the same design token workflows.

As part of this initiative, we launched a Figma plugin alongside a VS Code extension, giving designers and developers consistent tools while improving collaboration and design-to-development handoff.

Together, these tools enable system-wide design updates through design tokens, improving consistency, scalability, and development speed. The Figma plugin also brings documentation, templates, QA, and handoff support directly into Figma, helping designers stay aligned with Polaris while focusing on solving user problems.

Check out this stellar presentation at Figma Config 2022 by our very own highly talented Staff Product Designer, José Torre. He touches on why we should approach design systems like gardening, instead of architecture in a very insightful talk on misconceptions about a design system's role in the world of product design.
