The Hackathon for PositiveChange

As one of Canada’s largest hackathons, DeltaHacks brings together over 800 of the tech world’s most creative, enthusiastic, and passionate students at McMaster University for a phenomenal weekend. It's 36 hours of building across platforms to create hundreds of groundbreaking projects from scratch. Whether focused on healthcare, education, environment, or accessibility, participants strive to create meaningful impact through their ideas.

As Co-Director of DeltaHacks, I was responsible for the overall success of the event, overseeing strategy, operations, and team leadership. In previous years, I served as Design Lead, where I built and scaled the design function from the ground up. Across both roles, my focus was on creating a cohesive system that upheld the DeltaHacks brand while empowering creativity. Over an 8-month cycle, I led the team with the tools, structure, and direction needed to deliver a polished, memorable experience for attendees.
